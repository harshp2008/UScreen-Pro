`uscreen toggle-tablet` tells the running daemon to flip the pen between pressure-tablet and mouse routing for the focused app. It is the same as Ctrl+Alt+P with the GNOME extension; bind it to a key on desktops without the extension.

The options are in the [CLI reference](/UScreen-Pro/reference/uscreen-toggle-tablet/).
