## One tablet, three jobs

<div class="modes">
<div class="card reveal"><div class="ico"><svg class="i"><use href="#i-monitor"/></svg></div><h3>Second display</h3><p>A real extra monitor, streamed in hardware. Touch and pen come back.</p></div>
<div class="card reveal" style="--d:1"><div class="ico"><svg class="i"><use href="#i-pad"/></svg></div><h3>Touchpad</h3><p>Swipes, pinch and palm rejection. Nothing is streamed.</p></div>
<div class="card reveal" style="--d:2"><div class="ico"><svg class="i"><use href="#i-pen"/></svg></div><h3>Pen tablet</h3><p>Pressure, tilt and eraser on the screen you are already using.</p></div>

## What Pro adds

<div class="tiles">
<div class="tile reveal"><div class="ico"><svg class="i"><use href="#i-clip"/></svg></div><b>Pen menu</b><span>Cut, copy, paste from the pen</span></div>

<div class="tile reveal" style="--d:2"><div class="ico"><svg class="i"><use href="#i-pad"/></svg></div><b>Native gestures</b><span>Fingers replayed as a real touchpad</span></div>
<div class="tile reveal" style="--d:3"><div class="ico"><svg class="i"><use href="#i-zap"/></svg></div><b>Faster, smoother</b><span>Tuned HEVC and steady frame pacing</span></div>

<div class="tile reveal" style="--d:5"><div class="ico"><svg class="i"><use href="#i-wifi"/></svg></div><b>Wired or wireless</b><span>USB and Wi-Fi; Tailscale is experimental</span></div>
